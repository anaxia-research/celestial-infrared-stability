Abstract (plain text):
Soft theorems appear as poles and branch points of celestial Mellin amplitudes. We prove that no continuous rule recovers a soft residue from approximate Mellin data, even among positive completely monotone profiles, and that a uniform infrared modulus restores recovery with exact minimax rates. The rates cover finitely many noisy samples, angular data, every coefficient of a logarithmic principal part, and resummed branches of known or unknown exponent. Applications include Bondi memory, ordered soft limits, the leading massive QED charge and finite Mellin bands.
