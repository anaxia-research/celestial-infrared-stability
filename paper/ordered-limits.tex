\section{An exact diagnostic for ordered soft limits}
\label{sec:ordered}
Consecutive soft limits and shadow OPE prescriptions have established
analyses \cite{shadowstress,chargebracket}. The following scalar example
isolates a target-definition obstruction. It is not a physical scattering
amplitude or a new resolution of a celestial operator algebra.

For $x,y>0$, set
\[
 A(x,y)=\frac{e^{-x-y}}{y(x+y)},\qquad
 b(x,y)=xyA(x,y)=\frac{x}{x+y}e^{-x-y}.
\]
The stripped kernel is positive and bounded. With
$S_xA=\lim_{x\downarrow0}xA$ and the analogous operation in $y$,
\[
 S_y(S_xA)=0,\qquad S_x(S_yA)=1.
\]
Indeed, $S_xA=0$ for fixed $y>0$, whereas $S_yA=e^{-x}/x$.
For $\Re z,\Re w>0$, its double Mellin transform is exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mixeddivisor}
 G(z,w)=\int_0^\infty\!\int_0^\infty
 x^{z-1}y^{w-1}b(x,y)\,dx\,dy
 =\frac{\Gamma(z+1)\Gamma(w)}{z+w}.
\end{equation}
To prove the identity, substitute $u=x+y$ and $v=x/u$.
The integral separates into
$\Gamma(z+w)B(z+1,w)$, giving \eqref{eq:mixeddivisor}.
The meromorphic continuation consequently satisfies
\[
 \Res_{w=0}\big(\Res_{z=0}G\big)=0,\qquad
 \Res_{z=0}\big(\Res_{w=0}G\big)=1.
\]
For fixed $w\ne0$, $G$ is holomorphic at $z=0$; for fixed
$z\ne0$, its residue at $w=0$ is $\Gamma(z+1)/z$.
The mixed divisor $z+w=0$ prevents a common local Laurent expansion
whose only polar divisors are the two coordinate axes.

Any single number $q$ intended to approximate both ordered targets
must satisfy $\max\{|q|,|q-1|\}\ge1/2$, even with exact transform
data. Recovering an ordered pair has no such logical obstruction.
Thus a reconstruction prescription must specify an order, a physical
bracket, or assumptions that make the limits commute. Choosing an order
does not establish associativity or identify the appropriate physical
charge prescription. The cited charge-bracket construction supplies
additional physical input in its stated tree-level setting.
